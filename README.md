# 📊 Marketing Analytics Dashboard

An interactive **Power BI Marketing Analytics Dashboard** designed to analyze customer journeys, conversion performance, social media engagement, and customer reviews.

This project combines **SQL, Python, and Power BI** to transform raw marketing and customer data into interactive business insights that can help organizations understand customer behavior, measure engagement, monitor conversion performance, and evaluate customer sentiment.

---

# 📌 Project Overview

This project provides a comprehensive view of marketing performance through four major analytical areas:

- Overall Marketing Performance
- Conversion Analysis
- Social Media Engagement
- Customer Reviews & Sentiment

The dashboard allows users to explore performance across different products and time periods using interactive filters.

The project follows an end-to-end analytics workflow:

**Data → SQL → Python Enrichment → Power BI → Business Insights**

---

# 🎯 Business Problem

Marketing teams generate large amounts of data across customer interactions, social media activity, conversion journeys, and customer reviews.

Without proper analysis, it becomes difficult to answer important business questions such as:

- How effectively are visitors converting into customers?
- How does conversion rate change over time?
- Which products have better conversion performance?
- How many views, clicks, and likes are being generated?
- Which types of content receive the most engagement?
- How does customer rating change over time?
- What is the distribution of positive and negative customer sentiment?
- How many customer reviews are being received?
- What patterns can be identified from customer journeys and reviews?

Manually analyzing these datasets can make it difficult for businesses to identify trends and make timely decisions.

---

# 💡 Solution

This project integrates multiple datasets into an analytical workflow using SQL, Python, and Power BI.

The solution enables stakeholders to:

- Monitor overall marketing performance.
- Track conversion rates over time.
- Compare conversion performance across products.
- Analyze views, clicks, and likes.
- Understand social media engagement by content type.
- Analyze customer ratings and reviews.
- Identify customer sentiment patterns.
- Explore customer journeys and their actions.
- Filter analysis by product and time period.

The final Power BI dashboard provides an interactive environment for exploring marketing and customer behavior.
